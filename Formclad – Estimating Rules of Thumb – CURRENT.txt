FORMCLAD – ESTIMATING RULES OF THUMB – CURRENT
Version 1.1 | 23 September 2026
Decided by: Sofia (Commercial Approver), from four research sources and Formclad's existing Labour_Factors
Open to override by: Travis (Operations Approver) on any labour or buildability figure
Used by: the Estimate Review Agent, the Estimate Builder, and Labour_Factors in the Nexvia master

WHAT THIS IS
The knowledge an experienced Formclad estimator carries in their head, written down so estimates can be built and checked the same way every time: enough hours, enough material, nothing commonly missed.

HOW THE FIGURES WERE CHOSEN
Four research sources disagreed, in places by two to three times. One figure was picked for each item using these rules, in order:
  1. Keep Formclad's own figure where one exists and nothing shows it is wrong (the 0.17 hr/m2 sheeting rate is used on every tender).
  2. Prefer Australian manufacturer and NCC guidance over generic or international labour tables.
  3. Where sources still differ, take the middle, leaning cautious: under-allowing hours or material costs more than over-allowing.
  4. Keep the figures consistent with each other, so the line rates add up to believable whole-job totals.

TAGS
  CONFIRMED  Formclad's own established figure.
  WORKING    Decided 23 Sep 2026. Use it. Replace it when actual job results say otherwise.
Every figure here is a benchmark to check against, not a guarantee. The Job Results Register is what turns WORKING into proven.

Base conditions for every labour rate: 3–20 degree pitch, single storey, clear access, material craned to the working area.
Money figures (labour cost, ROR, margin, crane minimum) live in "Formclad – Pricing Settings – CURRENT" only. The $80/hr labour cost already includes normal productivity loss, so the hours below are not inflated for breaks.

4. DIFFICULTY LOADINGS
=====================================================================

LOADINGS MULTIPLY. Rate × 1.15 × 1.25, not rate × 1.40.   WORKING
  Each problem works on a job that is already less efficient, and multiplying is the cautious choice.

CAP: once the combined factor passes 1.60, stop loading and send the labour to the Operations Approver for a manual estimate.   WORKING

  Pitch over 20 degrees                         × 1.15   CONFIRMED
  Second storey or high reach                   × 1.15   CONFIRMED
  Cramped or internal box gutters               × 1.20   CONFIRMED
  Spring-curved on site                         × 1.15   WORKING
  Tight, segmented or pre-curved sheeting       × 1.40   WORKING (or manual estimate)
  Complex roof (many planes, hips, valleys, short runs)   × 1.25   WORKING (very complex × 1.40)
  Occupied building (school, live retail, operating plant)  × 1.15   WORKING
  Restricted hours (night, early, shutdown windows)  × 1.20   WORKING – wage penalties costed separately
  Regional job                                  × 1.00   WORKING – travel, accommodation, freight and mobilisation priced as Items
  Remote or logistically difficult regional     × 1.10   WORKING

  Example: 0.17 × 1.15 (occupied) × 1.25 (complex) = 0.244 hr/m2.

SMALL JOBS
  Under 100 m2, do not rely on m2 rates alone. Minimum 24 labour-hours (a crew of 3 for a day) wherever setup, induction, edge protection or lifting is needed.   WORKING


=====================================================================
5. WHOLE-JOB SANITY CHECKS
=====================================================================

These depend heavily on roof shape (a roof with lots of perimeter, hips and turn-ups has far more hours per m2), so they are a warning light, not a verdict. The line-by-line check is the real test.

  Total labour hours per m2 of roof
    Simple new roof                        0.28–0.40   (flag as LIGHT below 0.25)
    Re-roof including strip-out            0.50–0.75   (flag as LIGHT below 0.45)
  Total labour hours per m2 of wall cladding, including trims and subframe
    Standard exposed-fix                   0.30–0.40   (flag as LIGHT below 0.25)
    Architectural                          0.45–0.80+  (flag as LIGHT below 0.40)
  Above the range: comment on it, and name the lines driving it. Do not flag as a fault.

  Crew and programme
    Standard crew 3; 4 on large sheet-handling jobs; 2 on small or detail work.
    Productive hours for the programme check: 6.0 per person per day.
    Crew-days = total hours ÷ (crew × 6.0). Must fit the builder's programme; if not, the hours or the programme are a risk to price.

  Material : labour cost (information only – the ROR test matters more)
    New roofing about 1.5:1 to 2:1  |  re-roofing about 0.7:1 to 0.8:1  |  standard cladding about 1.5:1  |  architectural cladding about 0.5:1 to 1.5:1

  Past Formclad estimates: compare with two or three of similar type and size. Once the Job Results Register has entries, compare with actual results instead.

6. MATERIAL RULES
=====================================================================

WASTAGE AND ALLOWANCES
  Simple roof sheets                  measured area × 1.05     CONFIRMED
  Complex or triangular roof sheets   × 1.10 to 1.15           CONFIRMED
  Flashings and cappings              lineal metres × 1.10     CONFIRMED
  Fasteners and clips                 × 1.10                   CONFIRMED
  Sarking / membrane                  area × 1.10 (150 mm laps)   WORKING
  Insulation blanket                  area × 1.05, then round UP to whole rolls   WORKING
  Safety mesh                         area × 1.15, then round up to whole rolls   WORKING
  Rigid board                         area × 1.05              WORKING
  Wall sheets                         measured area × 1.05     WORKING (roof sheet rule applied to walls)
  Gutters, downpipes, fascia          lineal metres × 1.10     WORKING (flashing rule applied)
  Labour is always calculated on the NET measured quantity; allowances apply to material only. Efficaxone quantities usually already include waste – work back to net before applying these.
  A quantity exactly equal to the measured area means no allowance has been made.

COVER WIDTHS (Lysaght current data; check the specified product)
  Custom Orb / corrugated 762 mm  |  Trimdek / Trimclad 762 mm  |  Spandek / Metrospan 700 mm
  Klip-Lok 700 / Kingklip 700 700 mm  |  Klip-Lok 406 406 mm  |  Longline 305 305 mm
  Nailstrip, Snaplock, standing seam, interlocking, cassette, flat lock: use the nominated system's module – never a generic width.

SHEET LENGTHS
  Default is single lengths ordered to size. No automatic end-lap allowance. Where a roof needs an end lap or expansion joint (transport length, roof length, thermal movement), price it as its own item using the manufacturer's detail – especially on concealed-fix.

FLASHINGS
  Estimating stock length 6.0 m.
  Laps: roof 150 mm (250 mm exposed); vertical wall 100 mm (150 mm exposed).

FIXINGS AND CONSUMABLES
  Exposed-fix roof screws      5 per m2 + 10%   (an allowance, not the fixing specification)
  Concealed-fix roof           clips × fasteners per clip + 10% (about 1 clip per m2); do not use 5 per m2
  Face-fixed wall cladding     7 per m2 + 10%
  Rivets                       5 per lineal metre of flashing
  Sealant                      1 × 300 ml cartridge per 8 lineal metres of flashing
  Check that the quantity makes sense against the pack size: a 2,000 m2 roof needs about 11,000 screws.

GUTTERS AND DOWNPIPES
  Eaves gutter brackets        1.0 m centres, plus one at every stop end and corner (NCC maximum is 1.2 m; 1.0 m is Formclad's cautious default)
  Box gutter support           continuous, or boards/brackets at 600–750 mm
  Downpipe clips               1.2 m centres, at least 2 per drop

BOX GUTTER OVERFLOWS – MANDATORY CHECK
  Allow at least one overflow per box-gutter outlet, sump or rainhead as a placeholder.
  Every tender with box gutters carries this flag until answered: "Confirm box gutter overflow quantity and capacity against the hydraulic design and AS/NZS 3500.3."
  Box gutters over 15–20 m: expect two outlets, each with its own overflow.

IF THE ESTIMATE HAS NO LINE FOR THESE, ASK WHY
  Fasteners, clips, rivets, sealants, tapes
  Ridge, hip, barge and apron flashings where the roof shape needs them
  Valley gutters where there are valleys
  Stop ends, turn ups, turn downs
  Downpipe offsets, pops, clips
  An overflow for every box gutter outlet
  Sarking or membrane where the spec calls for it
  Safety mesh on new roofs


=====================================================================
7. COMMONLY MISSED ITEMS
=====================================================================

Each must be priced, excluded in the quote letter, or confirmed as not needed.

  Access and safety: crane or lifting; EWP or boom lift; scaffold or edge protection (or confirmation the builder provides it); anchor points or static lines; traffic management or footpath permits.
  Site and logistics: freight and delivery (and whether already in the supplier price); rubbish removal and disposal; strip-out; temporary weatherproofing on re-roofs; travel and accommodation; induction time; CBD parking or loading restrictions.
  Technical: shop drawings, engineering or design responsibility (box gutter sizing, wind certification); samples, mock-ups, warranties; after-hours or weekend work; making good to other trades; asbestos identified, excluded or subcontracted.
  Commercial: retention and security; liquidated damages; number of visits and return trips (each extra visit costs setup time).
  Pricing: any unit cost of $0 or $1, or any line described as "assume" or "TBC", is a placeholder until replaced.


=====================================================================
8. NOT USED BY THE AGENT – FOR THE DIRECTORS TO CONFIRM
=====================================================================

Money figures belong in Pricing Settings once approved. Until then the agent only checks that these appear as Items where the job needs them.
  Vehicle allowance $0.85–1.10 per km
  Accommodation and living-away allowance $180–240 per person per day
  Regional freight surcharge +10–25% on local freight
  Night or weekend penalty multiplier 1.5× to 2.0× the base rate

9. HOW THIS DOCUMENT IMPROVES
=====================================================================

  • Travis can override any WORKING figure at any time. Change it here and in Labour_Factors together, and log it below.
  • Every finished job: the Job Costing Review Routine compares quoted with actual hours. A rate that is out by more than 10% over three or more jobs gets changed and becomes CONFIRMED.
  • Every time Travis corrects an estimate the agent passed, add what he saw here.
  • Change one figure at a time so cause and effect stay visible.
