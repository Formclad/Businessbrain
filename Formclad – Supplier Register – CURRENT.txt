FORMCLAD – SUPPLIER REGISTER – CURRENT
Version 1.0 | 19 September 2026
Who we buy from, on what terms, and how reliable their pricing is. Seeded from the capability statement, tender pricing and the material libraries; fill the gaps as orders are placed.

WHY: every estimate starts with "what pricing do we actually hold for this?" A register answers that in seconds, and makes lead times visible before they become programme problems.

PRICING RULE (current practice, keep it)
Always obtain a quote for large quantities. Price books are often out of date, and several accounts have no current price book at all. Use price books for small quantities and for early budget pricing only, and note which is which in the estimate.
