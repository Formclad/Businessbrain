PAGE 2 – WHO WE ARE
=====================================================================

WHO WE ARE

Formclad is a specialist architectural roofing and cladding contractor working with architects, premium builders and small commercial clients across South Australia.

We are not generalists. We work on buildings where the roof and façade are part of the architecture: complex geometry, modern materials, exacting detail, and finishes that are meant to be looked at. That work needs a contractor who can read the detail, price it honestly, build it accurately and keep the programme intact.

The business is led by Travis Harding, who brings decades of hands-on commercial roofing and cladding experience to every project, and Sofia Calado, General Manager, who leads the commercial and delivery side of the business. Both are involved in the projects we take on, which is why our advice at design stage is practical rather than theoretical.

OUR PURPOSE
To elevate every project we touch through superior roofing and cladding that enhances architectural vision, withstands time, and sets the benchmark for technical craftsmanship.

OUR MISSION
To deliver architectural roofing and cladding with clarity, discipline and uncompromising standards, combining direct communication, tight organisation and collaborative problem-solving to create smooth, predictable, beautifully executed projects.

PAGE 3 – HOW WE WORK
=====================================================================

HOW WE WORK

Five commitments define how we work with every architect, builder and supplier. They are how we are measured internally, and what you should expect from us on site.

CLEAR AND CONFIDENT
We communicate early and plainly so your project stays predictable. You will know what is happening, what is next and what we need from you. Changes are flagged the moment we see them, not at the end of the month.

ORGANISED AND AHEAD OF IT
We prepare early, plan ahead and work to a defined process. Materials, access and labour are arranged before they are needed, so our work does not become the reason a programme slips.

RELENTLESS STANDARDS
We protect the detail, the finish and the design intent. Our work is checked and owned before anyone else sees it, and anything that is not right is corrected, including the small things.

STRAIGHT UP
Honest, fair and accountable. We give you a clear position on scope, cost, programme and risk, and we say so early when something will not work, rather than pricing around it.

BUILT ON COLLABORATION
We work as an extension of your team. We contribute solutions rather than friction, share information openly, and coordinate with other trades so the whole project moves together.

PAGE 4 – QUALITY, SAFETY AND DELIVERY
=====================================================================

QUALITY ASSURANCE AND ACCOUNTABILITY

We stand behind every installation:
  • 12-month workmanship guarantee on our installations, in addition to material warranties from our suppliers.
  • Premium, Australian-tested materials, including COLORBOND® and Zincalume® steel.
  • Finish standards checked on site before handover, against the detail and the design intent.
  • A solutions-focused approach to problems, so an issue reaches you with an option attached.

UNCOMPROMISING SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE

Safety underpins everything on site:
  • Project-specific Safe Work Method Statements for all high-risk activities.
  • Work carried out in strict accordance with the relevant Australian Standards for roof plumbing and metal cladding.
  • Fully licensed (BLD 333524) and insured for commercial work; certificates of currency available on request.
  • A safety-first culture that protects our team, your team and your programme.

DISCIPLINED DELIVERY

Our process is structured from first enquiry to final fix:
  • Early involvement at design stage, with technical advice on materials, systems and complex detailing.
  • Detailed take-off and transparent pricing, with assumptions, inclusions and exclusions stated plainly.
  • A defined handover from estimate to project budget, procurement and programme, so nothing is lost between quoting and building.
  • Weekly review of labour, costs, variations and programme while the job is live.
  • Prompt pricing and notification of variations, so surprises do not arrive at the end.
  • Structured close-out, with final documentation and lessons fed back into how we price and build the next one.

PAGE 8 – FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
=====================================================================

WHAT TYPES OF PROJECTS DO YOU SPECIALISE IN?
High-end architectural residential and small commercial work: metal roofing, wall cladding and façade installations where detail and finish matter. We take on selected remedial and re-roofing work where the scope and access are clear. We do not chase general maintenance.

HOW DO YOU WORK WITH ARCHITECTS AND BUILDERS AT DESIGN STAGE?
We prefer to be involved early. We give practical advice on material selection, system design and complex detailing, including where a detail will be difficult or costly to build, and what the alternatives are. Early input is usually cheaper than a variation later.

WHAT IS YOUR APPROACH TO SITE SAFETY AND COORDINATION?
Project-specific SWMS for all high-risk activities, work to the relevant Australian Standards, and a commitment to integrating with the master programme. We coordinate with other trades rather than working around them.

WHAT WARRANTIES AND GUARANTEES DO YOU PROVIDE?
A 12-month workmanship guarantee on our installations, alongside the material warranties provided by our suppliers, including COLORBOND® and Zincalume® steel.

ARE YOU LICENSED AND INSURED FOR COMMERCIAL WORK?
Yes. Licensed (BLD 333524) and insured for commercial and architectural projects across South Australia. Certificates of currency are available on request.

HOW DO YOU PRICE?
We price labour, materials, access and risk separately and transparently, and we state our assumptions, inclusions and exclusions. We would rather have a clear conversation about scope up front than a dispute about it later.
