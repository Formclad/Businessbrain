RULES FOR THIS FOLDER
- Keep one current version of each document, with CURRENT in the file name.
- Move older versions to the Superseded folder in this library (created 23 Sep 2026). Do not keep copies on desktops or in personal OneDrive folders.
- DOCUMENTS VERSUS LISTS (added 21 Sep 2026). Registers — repeating records with the same fields, such as Builder Profiles, the Supplier Register, published Client Grades, the job results register and the employee ticket register — belong in SharePoint lists, because lists can be filtered, queried by Power Automate and read properly by Copilot. Documents that carry sequence, reasoning or judgement stay as documents. The index points at the list; the list is the only copy. Never keep the same content in both a list and a file — that is how two sources of truth start.
- OPEN WORK VERSUS HOW THINGS RUN (added 22 Sep 2026). The same rule applied to the build itself. What is OPEN — to do, to decide, to supply, to consider — lives in the Planner plan (see BUSINESS SYSTEMS AND AGENTS). How the business RUNS lives here. When an item closes in Planner and changes how something works, write the change into the relevant document in this folder; ticking it in Planner is not the same as recording it.
- ONE HOME PER RULE (added 23 Sep 2026). Money figures live only in Pricing Settings. Estimating rates and allowances live only in Estimating Rules of Thumb. Who approves what lives only in Approval Authorities. Every other document — SOP, Commercial Rules, Nexvia master, QA Checklist, agent instructions — points to those three rather than restating their figures, so a change is made once.
- When using a file in a Copilot or Claude chat, work from the SharePoint copy and save the final result back to SharePoint before closing the chat. Personal OneDrive folders (Microsoft Copilot Chat Files, Copilot > Created) hold chat snapshots only and should be treated as scrap. Recorded 21 September 2026. Parts of this still need confirming.

THE CHAIN as currently understood: supplier invoice arrives at EzzyBills >
EzzyBills reads it > into Xero as a bill > exported > bulk-imported into
Nexvia > coded to a job and budget line.

THE PROBLEM WITH THAT SHAPE. The coding decision happens at the WRONG END.
By the time a bill reaches Nexvia's bulk import it has been through two
systems, it is often weeks old, and all anyone has to work from is a supplier
name and an amount. That is not data entry, it is forensics, and it is why
the job has kept landing on Sofia.

EzzyBills does not fix that. PURCHASE ORDERS do — see below. What EzzyBills
gives you is capture, an approval trail, and no typing.

PURCHASE ORDERS — THE PRACTICE
Set 21 September 2026. Supersedes "purchase orders: not currently used".
=====================================================================

WHY. Purchase orders are now raised UP FRONT for key suppliers, before the
bill arrives, so that an incoming bill matches its PO line and INHERITS the
coding. This removes the manual allocation step on every line of a bulk bill
import — the most tedious task in the accounts week.

RAISE THEM AT THE BUDGETED AMOUNT, NOT A NOMINAL $1. Confirmed 21 Sep 2026.
A purchase order is what Cost to Complete reads as COMMITTED cost. A $1 PO
commits $1, so the forecast shows nothing committed and every bill then
arrives as a surprise. Raising the PO at its budgeted figure gives both
benefits at once: the bill inherits the coding AND the committed number is
real from the day the order is placed. Coding is the smaller prize;
committed cost is the bigger one, and a nominal PO throws it away.

NOMINAL OR ALLOWANCE POs are for genuinely open-ended supply only — a hire
account, or a trade account taking many small deliveries where the total
cannot be known in advance. Even then use the budget allowance rather than
$1, so the commitment figure means something.

THE CODING HAS TO BE RIGHT. The PO must carry the assembly or item group
that matches the work packages. A PO coded to Miscellaneous inherits nothing
useful and simply moves the problem one step upstream. The three raised on
21 Sep are typed Assembly / Materials – Roofing & Cladding, which is correct.

TEST THIS BEFORE A BATCH. Confirm what Nexvia does when a bill EXCEEDS its
purchase order value in this configuration — whether it passes, flags a
variance, or forces an approval.

DO NOT SEND INTERNAL POs TO SUPPLIERS. A PO raised purely to carry coding is
an internal control, not an order document. Watch the "PO to be sent" count
on the staff dashboard.

WHO GETS ONE. Key suppliers first — the ones whose figures come straight off
the estimate. Record it against the Supplier Register (item 21).

Our Lady Queen of Peace.
