FORMCLAD – MONTHLY BRAIN REVIEW – CURRENT
Version 1.0 | 18 September 2026
15 minutes, in the first management meeting of the month. Owner: Sofia.

Purpose: keep the Brain true. A set of documents that no longer matches how the business works is worse than none, because people and AI tools both act on it.


THE SIX QUESTIONS

1. What changed this month that the Brain doesn't know?
   New people, new rates, a new builder, a changed process, a decision made in a conversation. Anything answered once verbally is a candidate for the Brain.

2. Where did a document disagree with what we actually did?
   The most valuable question. If someone worked around a process, the process is wrong or the document is out of date. Fix whichever it is; don't leave the gap.

3. Financial settings: approved?
   Reports saved into Financial Reports, Claude proposes the updated figures, Sofia approves or adjusts. Changes flow to Pricing Settings, the calculator and the index.

4. Client grades: anything to update?
   Jobs finished this month, payment behaviour, builders newly quoted. Update the ratings tool and republish the grades file.

5. Open items: what moved, what's stuck?
   Work through the to-do list in the index. Anything untouched for three months either gets a date or gets deleted — a permanent to-do list is just noise.

6. Version check: is anything drifting?
   One CURRENT version of each document? Any work saved to a desktop, a personal OneDrive or a chat folder instead of SharePoint? Old versions in Superseded?


QUARTERLY, ON TOP OF THE ABOVE
- Business plan Part A and Part B reviewed against actual results.
- Pricing assumptions checked against completed-job data: productive hours, labour cost, wastage allowances, difficulty factors.
- Approval authorities and thresholds still right for the size the business is now.
- Whether anything in the Brain is no longer used, and can go.

ANNUALLY
- Owner goals and the profit target.
- Client ratings, all of them.
- Capability statement and marketing alignment.
- Insurances, licences, tickets and registrations.


HOW TO RUN IT
Ask Claude for a Brain review before the meeting. Claude lists what changed, what's outstanding, and anything that looks stale or contradictory, so the 15 minutes is spent deciding rather than searching.

WHAT MAKES IT FAIL
- Turning it into a reading session. It's six questions and a decision on each.
- Letting the to-do list grow without ever cutting it.
- Updating a document without updating the index, so the next person reads something that no longer points anywhere.
