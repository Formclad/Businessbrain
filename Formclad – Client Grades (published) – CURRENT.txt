FOR THE PREQUALIFICATION AGENT AND ANYONE PRICING
- Check the client's grade before recommending PROCEED or NO BID, alongside scope, materials, storeys and distance.
- An unrated client is not the same as a poor one. Flag it for a provisional rating rather than assuming.
- A grade never overrides the labour return and margin gates in Pricing Settings. A grade-A builder with a job below the minimum return still needs commercial review.
- For a C client, include a risk allowance for slow payment, variation disputes and coordination problems rather than absorbing it.
