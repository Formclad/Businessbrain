EARLYTRADE — what it is, since several builders use it
Earlytrade is an early-payment platform. The builder (through Earlytrade) offers to pay a certified claim early in exchange for a small discount. Formclad has taken these offers before. Accepting one is signed through Synr, Earlytrade's signing platform, with a "Deed of Consistency" per claim.
Two things to know:
- Accepting an offer is a Commercial Approver decision: the discount is a cost against the claim, weighed against the cash benefit of being paid early.
- When reconciling RCTIs and remittances, subtract any Earlytrade payments already received. Otherwise the job looks underpaid (this caused confusion on Chappell's Goolwa job in 2025).
