FORMCLAD – APPROVAL AUTHORITIES – CURRENT
Who approves what, and what the older documents mean by "Director"

As at: 18 September 2026
Set by: Travis Harding and Sofia Calado
Review: annually, or whenever roles or the company structure change


1. WHY THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS

Formclad restructured from a family trust to a company. Titles changed; the people making the decisions did not. Several documents (the Tender & Estimating SOP, the Estimate Review Commercial Rules & Delegation, the business plan) use "Director" as the approval authority. This document defines the authorities by what they cover rather than by title, so approvals stay valid when titles change.


2. THE AUTHORITIES

COMMERCIAL APPROVER — Sofia Calado, General Manager
Covers: pricing policy and pricing settings, submission approvals on commercial grounds, margin and labour return exceptions, payment and cash decisions, contract terms and unusual commercial risk, credit and debtor decisions, supplier and subcontractor commercial terms, statutory, accountant and ATO matters, and process and systems ownership.

OPERATIONS APPROVER — Travis Harding, Director
Covers: buildability, scope and construction method, labour hours and productivity assumptions, programme and sequencing, site leadership and safety, procurement validation, variations arising on site, and client and builder decisions requiring construction judgement.

TENDER & ESTIMATING COORDINATOR — Mairead
Covers: everything delegated in the SOP: tender intake, prequalification outcomes, take-off coordination, supplier pricing, estimate preparation, and submission where the delegation conditions are met.

FINANCE ADMINISTRATION — Marianne
Covers: preparation, records and registers. Prepares information and exceptions for decision; does not approve payments, write-offs or credits.

BOTH APPROVERS TOGETHER
Some decisions need both a commercial and an operational view: taking on a project above the single-job size guide, any project that changes team capacity or requires new fixed cost, contract amendments that shift risk materially, and any decision to proceed below the reprice line.


3. READING THE OLDER DOCUMENTS

Where an existing document says:
  "Director review required" (on price, margin, ROR, contract terms, credit or cash) → Commercial Approver.
  "Director review required" (on scope, buildability, labour hours, programme, site or procurement) → Operations Approver.
  "Director commercial review" → Commercial Approver.
  "Escalate to Travis, copy Sofia" → unchanged; that is Operations Approver, copy Commercial Approver.
  "Directors" (plural, for example in the capability statement) → Travis Harding, Director, and Sofia Calado, General Manager.

This mapping applies until each document is updated in its own words. It does not change any threshold; it only names who holds the authority.
