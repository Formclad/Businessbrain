FORMCLAD – SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENTS – CURRENT
Version 1.0 | 18 September 2026
Owner: Commercial Approver (General Manager)

THE PROBLEM
Builders email subcontract agreements to Sofia or Travis. They are often not saved to the project folder or uploaded to Nexvia. The consequences show up later: nobody can check the payment terms when a claim is late, the retention percentage and release dates are unknown at close-out, notice periods for delays and variations are missed, and back charges arrive with no reference to what was agreed. A signed contract that exists only in an inbox is a contract nobody can use.

THE RULE
No subcontract agreement is signed until it has been reviewed, and no agreement is filed only in email. One destination, every time: the project folder, Nexvia, and a one-page summary of the terms that matter.


1. WHEN AN AGREEMENT ARRIVES
Whoever receives it (Sofia or Travis) does one thing immediately: save the file to the project or tender folder under 01 - Commercial, named "[Builder] – [Project] – Subcontract Agreement – UNSIGNED [date]". Thirty seconds, before anything else.
If the job does not have a folder yet, save it to the tender folder.

2. REVIEW BEFORE SIGNING (Commercial Approver)
Check and record these terms — they are what the rest of the business runs on:
[ ] Contract sum and what is included.
[ ] Payment terms: claim date each month, certification period, payment period, method (Nexvia email, PayApps, RCTI).
[ ] Retention: percentage, cap, release on practical completion, release at end of defects liability.
[ ] Defects liability period length.
[ ] Variation clause: how a variation is instructed, the notice period for claiming it, and whether verbal instructions count.
[ ] Delay and extension of time: notice period and what has to be included.
[ ] Liquidated damages: rate and cap.
[ ] Security or bank guarantee requirements.
[ ] Set-off and back-charge rights.
[ ] Insurance requirements and whether our cover meets them.
[ ] Warranty period required, against our 12-month workmanship guarantee.
[ ] Programme dates and any access assumptions we priced.
[ ] Scope attached matches what we quoted, including our stated inclusions, exclusions and qualifications.
Anything unusual or one-sided goes to both approvers before signing. Where our quote qualifications are not carried into the contract, say so in writing before signing, not after.

3. SIGNING
Signed by the Commercial Approver, or by Travis where the terms have already been reviewed and agreed.
Save the signed copy in the same folder as "…– Subcontract Agreement – SIGNED [date]".

4. INTO NEXVIA
[ ] Upload the signed agreement to the project's documentation.
[ ] Enter the contract sum, payment terms and retention in the project so claims and the retention register are built from the real terms.
[ ] Record the claim date each month for this builder.

5. THE CONTRACT SUMMARY (one page per job)
Fill this in once and it answers most questions for the rest of the job without anyone reopening the contract:
  Project | Builder | Contract sum | Claim date | Certification days | Payment days | Payment method
  Retention % | Retention cap | Release at PC | Release at end of DLP | DLP length
  Variation notice period | EOT notice period | LDs rate and cap | Security required
  Insurance requirements | Key exclusions and qualifications carried into the contract | Anything unusual
Save it beside the agreement and reference it in the close-out checklist.

6. WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE
Any of us can answer, in under a minute and without opening an inbox: what is the claim date, what are the payment terms, how much retention is held, when is it released, and how long do we have to notify a variation.
