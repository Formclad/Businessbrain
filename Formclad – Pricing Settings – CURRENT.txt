2. HOW LABOUR RETURN IS CALCULATED

ROR per budgeted labour hour = (quote total ex GST − materials − subcontractors − equipment/hire − other direct non-labour costs) ÷ budgeted labour hours.

Gross margin = (quote total ex GST − all direct costs including labour) ÷ quote total ex GST.

Material-heavy work gives a lower gross margin for the same labour return, so gross margin is a check alongside ROR rather than a stand-alone test.


3. HOW THESE FIGURES ARE SET AND UPDATED

The directors set these figures from the company's cost base, capacity and financial targets, and review them monthly against actual results. The underlying calculations are held confidentially by the directors.

When they change, the directors update this file, the labour return calculator and the Business Brain index together, so everyone is working from the same numbers.
